# WiCare – content rules

What the website may say, how it looks, and what must never change. Last updated: 18 Nov 2026.

## Positioning
- The site sells **private chef & catering**: private dinners at home, corporate catering, parties & celebrations, Christmas lunch.
- Cleaning (homes & businesses) and garden & construction are **"Øvrige services"** – shown, but never first.
- Area: the northern suburbs of Copenhagen – the 20 towns from Hellerup to Allerød. Not Humlebæk/Espergærde/Snekkersten.

## Claims – only what's true and confirmed
- Chef: **Wiktoria R.** – "13+ years of experience, including Michelin-starred restaurants and the Danish Parliament (sous-chef)". Say "our chef", never "Michelin-star chef".
- **No prices, no packages** – the price is always agreed with the customer.
- **No response-time promises** (no "we reply within 2 hours").
- **No opening hours** – "when it suits you, by arrangement – also evenings and weekends".
- Confirmed true: wine pairing, event planning, premium Danish suppliers, background-checked staff, project management.
- Occasions we may name: birthdays & anniversaries, confirmations, weddings, summer & garden parties, Christmas lunches.
- Not offered (never mention): dog walking, plant watering, handyman, car detailing, wardrobe/scenting/device cleaning extras, "licensed/autoriseret".
- No fake reviews or testimonials – only real, attributable ones (e.g. from Google).
- Photos: prefer real photos. The current chef hero images are AI-generated and should be replaced with real ones – never edit out the AI watermark to pass them off as real.

## Contact & booking
- Call-first: phone **+45 52 72 11 02**, WhatsApp, **hello@wicare.vip**. No contact form (owner's choice).
- **Printed business cards have a QR code to `https://wicare.vip/#booking`** – that anchor must stay on the top (hero) section. Never move it.
- "Book now" buttons go to `/#book` (the call section at the bottom).

## Language
- Danish is the default (what Google sees); English via the **EN / DK** switch (never write "DA" in the UI).
- Danish uses sentence case: "Klar til at komme i gang?" – not English Title Case. Exception: the 3-line hero "VIP / Privat kok / & catering".
- Every Danish text has an English twin with the same meaning.

## Brand
- Colours: navy `#1B2B4A`, steel blue `#6BA8CE` (text on light: `#2E6A93`), light `#F1F4F8`.
- Logo: the "WiCare" wordmark ("Wi" white/navy + "Care" steel blue).
- Fonts: Playfair Display (headings) + Inter (text), self-hosted.
- Hero motto: "Diskretion. Præcision. Tillid." on its own line (don't follow it with "betroet/trusted").

## Never bring back
- Critida / olive oil / "Partners" section.
- The old emerald/brass colours.
- Bright WhatsApp green (#25D366) buttons.
- Black button text.
- Placeholder testimonials, the 2h/100%/5-days stat tiles, the sample Christmas menu.
